BEFORE YOU FOLLOW THE RIVER

A few things to know.

Ask us anything↗
Where does the journey begin?

We are based in Puerto Nariño, in the Colombian Amazon. Leticia is the usual air gateway; onward travel to Puerto Nariño is by river. Confirm the departure time, boarding point and onward connections before buying tickets. Share your arrival plans with Amaru and ask where your experience begins and which transport, if any, is included in the proposal.

When should I visit?

The river changes throughout the year. Higher water can open routes through flooded forest; lower water offers different walking routes and river landscapes. Tell us your dates and we will explain what is possible at that time. Rain is part of the experience.

Can I arrange a guide in my language?

Let us know your preferred language when you enquire. We will confirm guide or interpreter availability and any additional cost before you book. The language of this website does not guarantee a guide in that language.

What should I bring?

Pack light: breathable long sleeves, long trousers, rain protection, suitable walking shoes, a hat, insect repellent and a refillable water bottle. Ask for the equipment list for your chosen outing and confirm what is provided. Internet access in Puerto Nariño can be limited: save useful travel details for offline use. Plan cash for local expenses and check accepted payment methods before relying on cards.

How do pricing and reservations work?

Send your dates, group size and interests on WhatsApp. We will discuss availability and send a personalised proposal with the total price, inclusions, payment conditions and cancellation terms. An enquiry is not a confirmed booking. No payment is collected on this website.

Is kayaking a daytime activity?

Yes. Our Kaya / kayaking experience takes place during daylight. Birdwatching and the cacao tour are separate experiences and can each be requested individually. Routes depend on water levels and weather.
